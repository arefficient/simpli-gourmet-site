When people ask what makes our Mac & Cheese a staple at every repass, gathering, and celebration, the answer comes down to three things: quality cheese, patience, and a generous touch of love.

### Ingredients
- 1 lb elbow macaroni
- 1/2 cup unsalted butter
- 1/2 cup all-purpose flour
- 4 cups whole milk & heavy cream blend
- 3 cups sharp cheddar cheese, freshly grated
- 2 cups colby jack cheese, freshly grated
- 1 cup smoked gouda for depth
- Salt, black pepper, garlic powder, and smoked paprika to taste

### Method
1. **Boil the Pasta:** Cook your elbow macaroni in heavily salted boiling water until al dente. Drain and toss lightly with butter to prevent sticking.
2. **Make the Roux:** Melt butter in a heavy-bottomed pot, whisk in flour, and cook for 2 minutes until golden and nutty.
3. **Build the Velvety Sauce:** Slowly whisk in your warm milk and cream mixture. Simmer until thickened and smooth.
4. **Melt the Cheeses:** Remove from heat and fold in three-quarters of your grated cheese blend, stirring until completely melted and luxurious.
5. **Bake:** Combine pasta with the cheese sauce, transfer to a baking dish, top with the remaining cheese and a dusting of smoked paprika. Bake at 375°F for 25 minutes until bubbly and golden brown on top.
