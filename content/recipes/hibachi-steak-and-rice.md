Hibachi isn't just a meal — it's an experience. The sizzle of the flat top, the aroma of garlic butter, and the lively energy of live cooking turn any gathering into a celebration.

### The Essentials
- High-heat flat top grill (or a well-seasoned cast iron skillet)
- Garlic butter (lots of it!)
- Soy sauce, sesame oil, and teriyaki glaze
- Fresh vegetables: zucchini, onions, mushrooms, and broccoli
- Tender chicken, shrimp, or thinly sliced steak

### The Secret Sauce
No hibachi night is complete without homemade **Yum Yum Sauce**. 
Mix mayonnaise, melted butter, a splash of water, tomato paste, garlic powder, paprika, a pinch of sugar, and a dash of cayenne pepper. Whisk until perfectly smooth and creamy.

### Chef's Tip
Prep all your ingredients before you turn on the heat. Once the flat top is hot, cooking goes fast!
