Candied yams are a cornerstone of soulful comfort food. The key is slow-simmering and baking until the sauce reduces into a rich, glossy caramel syrup that coats every single slice.

### Ingredients
- 4 large sweet potatoes, peeled and sliced into thick rounds
- 1 cup packed dark brown sugar
- 3/4 cup unsalted butter
- 1 tbsp ground cinnamon
- 1 tsp freshly grated nutmeg
- 1 tsp pure vanilla extract
- A pinch of sea salt and a handful of mini marshmallows (optional finish)

### Method
1. Arrange sweet potato slices neatly in a buttered baking dish.
2. In a saucepan, melt the butter, brown sugar, cinnamon, nutmeg, and vanilla together until bubbling.
3. Pour the warm spiced syrup evenly over the sweet potatoes.
4. Cover tightly with foil and bake at 350°F for 45 minutes. Remove foil, baste generously with the syrup, and bake uncovered for another 20 minutes until tender and caramelized.
